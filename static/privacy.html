say2 is a hackathon project in active development. It is offered as is, for testing and demos.
01

What we read

When someone sets up an assistant, we read up to 50 public pages of that website, including public PDFs. We only read pages anyone can open without logging in. We don't read pages behind logins, and we respect sites that block automated readers.

02

What we store

  • The text of those public pages, the site's logo link, name and description, and links for booking or ordering.
  • The style settings chosen at setup: voice, color, look and signature.
  • A scrambled version of the owner's edit key. We never store the key itself.
  • What the owner adds in Back office: their own answers and house rules.
03

Chats

We don't save chat conversations. When a visitor asks a question, the question and the site's stored text are sent to the AI model to write the answer. Recent messages are kept only in the visitor's open browser tab so the assistant can follow the conversation.

One exception: when the assistant can't answer a question, we save that question's text (nothing else, no name or contact) so the business owner can answer it in their Back office. Please don't type personal details into the chat.

04

In your browser

A few small settings live only in your own browser, never on our server:

Owner edit keyLight or dark choice

Clear your browser's site data and they're gone. If you're an owner, that also means losing access to your Back office from that browser.

05

Who processes the data

Answers are written by NVIDIA's Nemotron model, running on Nebius Token Factory. Questions and site text are sent there to create each answer. The app itself is hosted on Render.

06

Answers can be wrong

The assistant answers only from the business's own website and links to its source, but AI can still make mistakes, and websites go out of date. Please confirm anything important with the business directly, especially allergies, prices and policies. The business is responsible for what its website says.

07

Fair use

  • Set up assistants only for websites you own or have permission to use.
  • Don't use say2 to spam, overload or misuse anyone's website or our service. We limit how often anyone can use it.
08

Removing a site

If you own a website and want its assistant and stored text removed, open an issue on our GitHub page and we'll delete it.
