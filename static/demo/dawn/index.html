Our story

From two kitchens to one counter.

Dawn & Drizzle started in 2019, when Meera and Daniel put her family's filter coffee next to his grandmother's honey cake and the line went out the door. Today we roast Araku Valley beans every Tuesday, bake from scratch every morning, and drizzle local wildflower honey on just about everything.
