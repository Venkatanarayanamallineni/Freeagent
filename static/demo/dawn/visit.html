The kitchen stops taking food orders 30 minutes before we close. We're closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Good to know

Parking: free 2-hour street parking on Church Street, plus a free public lot on Elm Avenue, one block north.

Wifi: free for customers. The password is printed on your receipt.

Laptops: welcome on weekdays. On Saturday and Sunday from 9 am to 1 pm we keep tables laptop-free for brunch.

Patio: open April through October, weather permitting. Dogs are welcome on the patio and we keep water bowls out. Inside, service animals only.

Accessibility: step-free entrance from Church Street and an accessible restroom.

Seating: about 40 seats inside and 16 on the patio.

Delivery: we don't deliver regular orders. Catering orders can be delivered, see Catering & events.
